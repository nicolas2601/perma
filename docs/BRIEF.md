## What PERMA is
Research prototype for image-assisted dermatology exam: clinician loads an image, inspects it, fills a manual ABCDE review, compares, annotates, exports a summary. Model output is experimental raw scores: it does NOT diagnose and is not clinical validation. Persistent notice: "Prototipo experimental de investigación. Los puntajes no son probabilidades clínicas. No diagnostica."
